## SEA guidance
Do not send all Google Ads traffic to the homepage. Match ad group and ad copy to the closest landing page. Keep private sailing holidays, group sailing, sailing instruction, professional skipper hire and yacht delivery as separate commercial intents.

## Copy principle
Search language opens the door. Task-B's own brand language — **Sailing for curious people**, **Structured spontaneity**, **Task-B skipper**, **Blue Book**, and **Orange Chapter** — creates differentiation after the click.
