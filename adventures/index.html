Private skipper-led sailing holidays

Go further than a charter week.

Task-B builds private sailing adventures around your group — combining real sailing, flexible itineraries, local exploration and a skipper who actively shapes the week with you.

Plan your adventureExplore Croatia

A yacht is not a hotel with a mast.

It is a way to reach places, change plans and move through a region differently.

A Task-B sailing holiday can be active, relaxed, instructional, social or all four in the same week. Sail hard when the wind is good. Stay longer when an anchorage earns it. Go ashore for food, history, swimming, a hike or simply because somebody heard about something worth finding.

For current operations, suitable yachts are selected through professional charter partners according to group size, comfort, destination and sailing objectives.

Real sailing

The boat is part of the adventure.

When conditions allow, we sail properly. Guests can participate or relax, but the yacht is not treated as floating accommodation that happens to have sails.

What shapes the week

One coherent trip

Bigger sailing regions

Range creates freedom.

Task-B favours capable sailing yachts and itineraries that can cover meaningful distance. One-way routes, different embarkation and disembarkation ports, relocation weeks and broader regional journeys become possible when the yacht is allowed to move.
