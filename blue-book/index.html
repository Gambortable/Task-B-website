The Task-B Blue Book

Knowledge worth sailing for.

A living internal knowledge base of anchorages, restaurants, food, hikes, history, weather, marina notes, local contacts and discoveries across the regions Task-B sails.

Never finished.

The Blue Book is how Task-B turns individual skipper knowledge into company knowledge.

It can contain practical sailing notes and provisioning information, but also the things that make a trip memorable: a family restaurant, the quiet side of a village, a swim before breakfast, a market worth arriving early for, a story behind an island or a person worth calling when the group wants something unusual.

Not every Blue Book entry will be published. The point is that every skipper contributes and every trip leaves something useful behind.
