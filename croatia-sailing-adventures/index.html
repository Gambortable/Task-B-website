Private sailing holidays Croatia

Explore the Adriatic properly.

Private skipper-led sailing adventures in Croatia for groups who want real sailing, flexible itineraries, island exploration, local food and more freedom than a standard charter loop.

Plan Croatia

Sail beyond the obvious route.

Croatia rewards curiosity: hundreds of islands, short passages, long passages, historic towns, quiet bays, strong local food culture and enough variety to build very different weeks in the same sailing region.

Task-B uses weather, group interests and local knowledge to choose between possible routes rather than treating one charter base as the centre of every trip.

Depending on yacht availability and logistics, journeys can start or finish in different ports and can include the central Dalmatian islands around Brač, Hvar, Šolta, Vis and the Šibenik archipelago.
