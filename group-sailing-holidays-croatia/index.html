Private group sailing holidays Croatia

One group. One yacht. Your week.

Private sailing holidays for friends, families, couples, reunions and celebrations — with a professional skipper and enough flexibility to make the trip feel like yours.

Discuss your group

Better when everyone already has a reason to be together.

Task-B increasingly focuses on whole-yacht private groups rather than selling individual berths.

That gives the skipper freedom to learn what the group enjoys, adjust the sailing, choose the right balance between anchorages and time ashore and build one coherent atmosphere onboard.

The yacht setup is selected around group size, comfort, budget, sailing objectives and destination. For larger groups, multi-yacht concepts can also be considered.

The social side

The boat brings people together.

Shared sailing, swimming, a long lunch ashore or a quiet evening at anchor all work differently when the group already knows one another. The skipper's job includes reading that dynamic rather than standing apart from it.

Participation is optional

Do as much — or as little — as you want.

Some guests want to sail every mile. Others want the helm for an hour and then a book in the cockpit. A private group lets the week adapt naturally.
