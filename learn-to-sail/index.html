Practical sailing instruction, own-boat tuition and informal learning during Task-B holidays. Build real confidence with the yacht, not just knowledge for a test.

People should understand more about sailing after a Task-B trip than when they arrived.

That can be informal — taking the helm, trimming a sail or working through an anchoring plan — or it can be focused private tuition around manoeuvring, seamanship, navigation and yacht handling.

Task-B can also facilitate structured training and certification through appropriate recognised training organisations where required.

Practical tuition

Confidence comes from repetition.

Docking, anchoring, sail handling, route planning and crew communication become easier when they are taught in context and then repeated until they feel normal.
